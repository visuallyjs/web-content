What will you build today?

Node based applications

Create components to render rich content and interactivity in your nodes and groups. Wire them up with a view and apply an automatic layout. VisuallyJs makes it a snap to build callflow designers, chatbot builders, kanban boards, automation flows, and so much more.

[![When you've reached the limits with ReactFlow, VisuallyJs has what you need](https://static.visuallyjs.com/img/app-card/callflow-2400.png)](/demonstrations/callflow.md)

[Call Flow](./demonstrations-callflow.md)

[![VisuallyJs - industry standard diagramming and rich visual UI Javascript and Typescript library](https://static.visuallyjs.com/img/app-card/flowchart-2400.png)](/demonstrations/flowchart.md)

[Flowchart](./demonstrations-flowchart.md)

Diagrams

Render SVG shapes using shape libraries. Shrink, grow and rotate using our resizing tools. Drag new shapes onto your canvas from a palette. Everything you need to build world-class diagrams is available out of the box. Build flowcharts, BPMN editors, call flow builders, SCADA/HMI consoles, process flow diagrams, ERD editors, mindmaps and much more.

Charts

Full range of 2D charts - column, bar, area, line, scatter - plus pie charts, gauges and more specialized charts such as sankey.

Populate charts from standalone data or use the data model from an app or diagram for dynamically updated insights into your dataset.

[![When you've reached the limits with ngDiagram, VisuallyJs has what you need](https://static.visuallyjs.com/img/app-card/area-line-chart-2400.png)](/demonstrations/area-line-chart.md)

Dashboards

Create a model and use it as the single source of truth for a mix of apps/diagrams and charts. The possibilities are endless.

All the plugins

Miniview

![](https://static.visuallyjs.com/img/plugins/miniview-500.png)

A miniaturized view of your canvas. Pan/zoom via the miniview, and click to scroll

Lasso

![](https://static.visuallyjs.com/img/plugins/lasso-500.png)

Snag a set of nodes and add them to the model's current selection.

Resize Tools

![](https://static.visuallyjs.com/img/plugins/resize-500.png)

Grow, shrink and rotate vertices, either with visible handles or via the vertex edges

Backgrounds

![](https://static.visuallyjs.com/img/plugins/background-500.png)

Grids - with dots or lines - and tiled or static images

Controls

![](https://static.visuallyjs.com/img/plugins/controls-500.png)

Buttons for common operations such as zoom to extents, undo/redo, etc

Edge Crossings

![](https://static.visuallyjs.com/img/plugins/line-crossings-500.png)

Mark the intersection of two edges with a bridge or a dot

Drawing

![](https://static.visuallyjs.com/img/plugins/vertex-drawing-500.png)

Draw new nodes/groups by dragging the mouse

Snaplines

![](https://static.visuallyjs.com/img/plugins/snaplines-500.png)

Visual cues to help align everything just so

Pan Buttons

![](https://static.visuallyjs.com/img/plugins/panbuttons-500.png)

Click to nudge the canvas, or hold to scroll

Scrolling Lists

![](https://static.visuallyjs.com/img/plugins/scrolling-lists-500.png)

Automatically park edges on their container when the source/target element is scrolled out of view

and many more...
