# Starter apps, diagrams and charts

#### What will you build today?

* For apps with rich content and interactivity in CSS styled nodes, take a look at our [Node-based applications](#apps).
* For pure SVG diagrams such as flowcharts etc, check out the [Diagrams](#diagrams).
* In the [Charts](#charts) section you'll find several demonstrations showcasing the wide range of charts that are available.
* To get a feel for how you can easily mix components to create powerful and compelling experiences, browse our [Dashboards](#dashboards).
