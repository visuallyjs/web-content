## Why choose VisuallyJs over the alternatives

### No Watermarks

Unlike other popular BPMN libraries like bpmn-js, VisuallyJs is a professional commercial product that does not force a watermark onto your canvas. Your application remains your own, branded exactly how you want it.

### Unbeatable Value

VisuallyJs provides the same (and often better) performance and flexibility as JointJS, but at 3 times less the cost. We believe in providing enterprise-grade tools at a price that makes sense for your business.

## Who builds BPMN tools with VisuallyJs

Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN) is the global standard for process modeling. VisuallyJs provides the robust foundation required to build enterprise-scale BPMN tools.

### Business Process Management (BPM) Suites

Comprehensive platforms for modeling, executing, and monitoring business processes. VisuallyJs provides the modeling canvas that integrates directly with your execution engine's XML or JSON definitions.

### Workflow Automation Platforms

Tools for automating repetitive tasks and orchestrating complex workflows across multiple systems. VisuallyJs handles the complexity of branching logic and nested sub-processes with ease.

### Enterprise Architecture Tools

Software for mapping out organizational structures and business capabilities. VisuallyJs's support for pools and lanes makes it ideal for visualizing how processes cross department boundaries.

### Compliance & Audit Documentation

Solutions for documenting standard operating procedures (SOPs) to meet regulatory requirements. VisuallyJs ensures your diagrams are standard-compliant and easy to export for reports.

## BPMN Features out of the box

### Full BPMN 2.0 Symbol Set

Tasks, gateways, events, and data objects. VisuallyJs includes a comprehensive library of standard-compliant BPMN shapes.

### Pools and Lanes

Visualize organizational boundaries and responsibilities with first-class support for pools and lanes, including automatic resizing and element containment.

### Automated Process Layout

Complex processes can be difficult to organize manually. VisuallyJs includes layout engines specifically tuned for business process flows.

### Standard XML/JSON Support

Easily import and export your diagrams to standard formats for interoperability with other BPMN tools and execution engines.

### Extensive plugin library

Accelerate development with 15+ pre-built plugins: miniviews, snaplines, undo/redo, automatic edge routing, and more.

### Interactive Property Inspectors

Attach technical metadata to your process steps—like script tasks or service endpoint configurations—using integrated inspectors.

### Framework-agnostic

VisuallyJs works with React, Angular, Vue, Svelte and TypeScript. Integrates cleanly into your existing application architecture without constraints - but offers a range of tightly integrated components to help you build professional applications for your target library with ease.
