## What are Area and Line charts and how can I develop them with VisuallyJs?

Area charts and Line charts are two fundamental visualization tools for displaying data over time or categories. A Line chart uses points connected by straight line segments to show trends and changes, providing a clear view of data fluctuations.

An Area chart is a variation of the line chart where the space between the line and the axis is filled with color or shading. This fill emphasizes the volume or magnitude of the data, making it ideal for visualizing cumulative totals or relative proportions between multiple datasets.

VisuallyJs provides a powerful way to build interactive Area and Line charts. By leveraging its robust data visualization capabilities and deep integration with popular UI frameworks, you can create dynamic charts that are not only visually appealing but also highly performant and responsive to user interactions.
