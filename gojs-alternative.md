# Why choose VisuallyJs over GoJS?

✓

VisuallyJs is flexible and composable and integrates fully into your HTML/CSS. GoJS draws to a canvas, insulated from the look and feel of your page

✓

With VisuallyJs you can use Angular, React, Vue or Svelte components to render your nodes with rich content and interactivity. In GoJS there is no way to do this.

✓

VisuallyJs ships with a comprehensive component testing manager

✓

VisuallyJs has an underlying graph at its core which you can interact with, to test for paths from some vertex to another, check if something is connected, etc

✓

VisuallyJs supports transactions - you can batch several operations on your model and undo/redo them all at once.

...and much more. If you can't build it with

<!-- -->

GoJS

<!-- -->

, you can with VisuallyJs!
