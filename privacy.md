# Privacy Policy

### Updated May 12th, 2026

This following document sets forth the Privacy Policy for VisuallyJs (https\://visuallyjs.com and subdomains).

VisuallyJs is bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), which sets out a number of principles concerning the privacy of individuals.

* #### Collection of your personal information

  Personal information collected is limited to the email addresses of individuals or groups within an organisation that contact VisuallyJs.

* #### Sharing of your personal information

  We do not share any personal information with any other company. We do not use any third-party sites to provide services on our behalf that require access to the personal information we collect.

* #### Use of your personal information

  We use your personal information for the purposes of establishing and maintaining relationships with evaluators and licensees of our software. From time to time, we may use customer information for new, unanticipated uses not previously disclosed in our privacy notice. If our information practices change at some time in the future we will use all personal information collected prior to our information practice updates in the manner outlined on this page in our July 12th 2019 update.

* #### Changes to this Privacy Policy

  VisuallyJs reserves the right to make amendments to this Privacy Policy at any time. If you have objections to the Privacy Policy, you should not access or use the Site.

* #### Accessing Your Personal Information

  You have a right to access your personal information, subject to exceptions allowed by law. If you would like to do so, please let us know. You may be required to put your request in writing for security reasons. VisuallyJs reserves the right to charge a fee for searching for, and providing access to, your information on a per request basis.

* #### Contacting us

  Visuallyjs welcomes your comments regarding this Privacy Policy. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy and would like further information, please contact us at hello\@visuallyjs.com.
